Supporting Be

Be is free. There is no price, no premium tier, no advertising and no sale of anything about you.

It is kept alive the way a hive is: by many small, freely given contributions.

If you would like to help

  • Money, when you have some to spare, through the donation links in the app and on the site. Any

amount, once or on a schedule you choose. Stopping needs no reason.

  • Time. Read a finding and tell us where the plain words went wrong. Try the app with a screen

reader. Translate a card into your language.

  • Attention: tell one person, if you think they would like it.

What the money does

Hosting for the site, and for the circle service when it exists. The small fees a payment processor

takes. The hours of the people who tend the code and the words. Accounts are published once a year

in plain numbers.

What support never buys

No feature is behind a donation. No donor is named in the app or ranked. Be never asks twice in one

session, never asks at a hard moment, and never counts who declined.

Not yet is an honored answer

Close the card. Nothing changes, and Be does not remember that you did.
