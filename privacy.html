What stays with you

Be's data practices, in plain words. This page is part of the app and works offline too.

What Be keeps, and where

  • Today's answer ("I'll try it" or "Not today"), your switches, and plain counts of what you tried, rested from, or settled — stored only in this device's local storage.
  • No account, no sign-in, no cookies beyond that local storage, no advertising, no analytics, no tracking of any kind.

What can leave your device

  • Nothing goes to anyone else. The only network code in Be is a service worker that fetches Be’s own files from wherever you installed it — same origin only, nothing third-party. There is no server to send your data to, and no sharing service exists.
  • The "Let my circle see how I'm doing" switch in You is a placeholder for work that has not been built. Turning it on changes what the app says, not what it does.
  • When sharing is built, this page will say exactly what leaves, and it will say it before anything moves.

The bee

A separate build of Be, served as bee.html, carries one optional link out: the bee, under the finding in Today. It is closed until you tap Ask, and every message is its own ask.

What leaves, once, when you tap Send: the words you typed, and the id of the finding on the page. That is the whole of it. The request body is {"v":1,"finding":"breath","text":"your words"} and nothing else. No counts, no choices, no switches, no name, no device id.

Where it goes: the bee’s host, named in the preview before you send. Today that is the Mac that hosts it, on the same Wi-Fi as your phone, reached with a code you typed once (or the address this build carries). The host keeps no copy of your words; it keeps an ordinary access log (address, time, status). The reply is shown once and is never saved on your device.

The bee is a model, and it can be wrong. Its replies are checked against Be’s voice rules before they are shown; a reply that fails is not shown.

The default build, app.html, has no bee and no network code at all. Nothing on this page about the bee applies to it.

What never leaves

Words, notes, names, and times. Be keeps none of these, so none exist to share, sell, or lose.

Taking it with you, or being forgotten

  • You → "Take my counts with me" saves a small text file of your counts to your device.
  • You → "Ask Be to forget everything" clears everything Be holds, at once, after one plain confirmation. Removing the app does the same.

What Be reaches for

The app is sealed into one file — fonts and code included — so it makes no network requests of its own while you use it. Your browser may fetch this app's files when you first open or update it.

Be offers reminders from research, plainly told. It isn't medical advice, and it never rushes you.

When hosting changes this

Whoever hosts Be for you may keep ordinary server logs (the address that fetched the files, and when). If a circle service is added, this page must be updated before it is switched on — the default stays no.
