Held, beyond Be

Some help is a person. These lines are free, and always there. The same list lives inside the Be app, offline; every entry was read on its own site on the date shown.

United States

988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline

Call or text 988

Free, confidential, every hour of every day. Chat at 988lifeline.org. (checked 2026-09-30, source)

Crisis Text Line

Text HOME to 741741

Free, confidential, any hour, anywhere in the United States. (checked 2026-09-30, source)

211

Call 211

Food, housing, bills, health, care for a caregiver. A person answers and points you to what is near. (checked 2026-09-30, source)

Find A Helpline

findahelpline.com

Free helplines by country and topic, for anywhere else in the world. (checked 2026-09-30, source)

Bay Area, California

East Bay Regional Park District

ebparks.org

73 parks and 1,330 miles of trail across Alameda and Contra Costa counties. Fees vary by park; the park page says. (checked 2026-09-30, source)

Berkeley Public Library

berkeleypubliclibrary.org

Central library and branches, most days 10 to 6, some evenings. Quiet, warmth, books, and a tool lending library. (checked 2026-09-30, source)

Berkeley Free Clinic

Call (510) 548-2570

830 University Ave. Never a fee: screening, peer counseling, benefits enrollment, glasses. Hours are limited; call first. (checked 2026-09-30, source)

Alameda County Community Food Bank

Call 510-635-3663

Food near you. The helpline answers weekdays, 9 to 4. A map of places is at foodnow.net, any hour. (checked 2026-10-13, source)

Long Island, New York

Long Island Crisis Center

Call (516) 679-1111

Any hour, every day. Chat at longislandcrisiscenter.org, seven in the morning to eleven at night. (checked 2026-10-13, source)

Island Harvest

Call 631-873-4775

Food pantries and distributions across Long Island; the site finds one near you. For help with food benefits, call 516-805-1642. (checked 2026-10-13, source)

Hempstead Public Library

hempsteadlibrary.info

115 James A. Garner Way. Monday to Thursday 10 to 9, Friday 10 to 6, Saturday 9 to 5. Quiet, warmth, books. (checked 2026-10-13, source)

Jones Beach State Park

Call (516) 785-1600

Six and a half miles of ocean beach at 2400 Ocean Parkway, Wantagh. A vehicle fee most days; the park page says. (checked 2026-10-13, source)

Jamaica

Mental Health Helpline, Ministry of Health and Wellness

Call 888-639-5433

The ministry line for mental health. The page does not give the hours; Find A Helpline above lists more lines in Jamaica. (checked 2026-10-13, source)

Jamaica Library Service

jls.gov.jm

Parish library networks; the site lists each library with its hours. Questions by WhatsApp: 876-275-0396. (checked 2026-10-13, source)

Blue and John Crow Mountains National Park

jcdt.org.jm

Holywell, Portland Gap and the Blue Mountain Peak Trail, kept by the Jamaica Conservation and Development Trust. A World Heritage Site since 2015. Call 876-960-2848. (checked 2026-10-13, source)
